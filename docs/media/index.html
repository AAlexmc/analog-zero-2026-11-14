The 30-second pitch

The challenge, the idea and the whole tour, with voice.

1 · Start from the mission

Pick a NASA mission objective on the Moon or on Mars. The spots A–E mark where that test fits.

2 · The whole Earth, parameter by parameter

The five closest places, the metrics, the match of every cell, and the portfolio.

3 · An analog is a place and a month

The match of each place in each of the 12 months, and the calendar to repeat a test.

4 · The base on both worlds

Rank what the base needs most, lay it out on both worlds, and see which published limit breaks.

5 · The real Sun, hour by hour

The day on both worlds with terrain shadows, the sheet of each piece, and the ground view.

6 · Equipment and drill

What you plant on one world appears on the other. The TRIDENT drill, bite by bite.

7 · The full report

Charts, base plan, limits and sources, for the Moon and for Mars, in one file.

Clips 1 to 7 have no sound. The voice of the pitch is a synthetic voice reading a script written by the team.
